Lamb da Herd is a 1v1 local multiplayer game made in Unity, where two rival shepherds compete to lure sheep into their own farm and score points.

Armed with grenades, you'll need to carefully position your throws and use the chaos to guide the flock toward your side of the field. Outsmart your opponent, capture more sheep, and bring home the win!

<strong>Gameplay</strong>

  Each player controls a shepherd on opposite sides of the field, player 1 uses the keyboard and mouse and player 2 uses a gamepad.
  
  Your goal is simple:
  
  Get more sheep into your farm than your opponent.
  
  Players can throw grenades around the field to influence the movement of nearby sheep. Use them strategically to:
  
  Lure sheep toward your farm
  
  Disrupt the flock
  
  Score points by getting sheep "safely" into your farm
  
  The game takes place on a field loosely inspired by a soccer pitch, with each player defending one side and trying to bring the flock home.

<strong>Features</strong>

  1v1 local multiplayer
  
  Throwable grenades
  
  Player farms and scoring zones
  
  Arena inspired by a soccer field
  
  Competitive, strategy-focused gameplay
  
  Designed around short, chaotic matches

Built With Unity and C#

<strong>How to Play</strong>

  From the Unity Project
  
  Clone the repository.
  
  Open the project using the appropriate version of Unity (Game was done using version 6000.0.56f1).
  
    Option 1:
    
      Open the main game scene.
      
      Press Play.
      
    Option 2:
    
      Build it and play

<strong>Objective</strong>

  Get sheep into your farm to score points.
  
  The flock won't simply cooperate, though. Use your grenades to manipulate the sheep's movement while trying to prevent your opponent from doing the same.
  
  The shepherd with more points wins!
  
  About the Project
  Lamb da Herd was created as a Unity game project focused on experimenting with local multiplayer gameplay, physics, and player-driven interactions.
  
  The project explores how simple mechanics can create unpredictable and competitive gameplay when multiple players are fighting over the same group of AI-controlled sheep.

<strong>Repository</strong>

  This repository contains the Unity project files, game scripts, scenes, assets, and other resources used to create Lamb da Herd.
